# Ogbifọ — Product Vision

## Vision

Build a living language intelligence platform that allows the Yorùbá language, its oral traditions, dialects, stories, proverbs, expressions, and speech patterns to be encountered, understood, practiced, contributed to, and preserved.

## The problem

Language tools frequently reduce language to translation.

That loses:

- context
- tone
- cultural meaning
- dialect
- oral rhythm
- provenance
- community authority

Ogbifọ approaches language differently.

The historical Ogbifọ was not merely someone who substituted one word for another. The Ogbifọ operated between worlds and interpreted meaning.

The digital product should perform the same role.

## Product thesis

> Translation is only one part of understanding.

Ogbifọ combines:

- language learning
- interpretation
- speech interaction
- cultural context
- community contribution
- peer verification
- archival preservation

## Target users

### Learner

A person learning Yorùbá.

Needs: authentic material, understandable translations, pronunciation practice, progressive difficulty, repetition.

### Native speaker

A fluent speaker who wants to: contribute material, preserve expressions, verify community submissions, represent dialects, earn recognition.

### Cultural researcher

Needs: provenance, language metadata, dialect information, verified material, searchable archives.

### Curious listener

May know little about the language but wants to encounter authentic language through accessible experiences.

## Product promise

Ogbifọ should make users feel:

> "I am not merely translating this language. I am entering it."

## Success

Ogbifọ succeeds when:

1. users repeatedly return to encounter language;
2. users develop measurable language/speech competence;
3. native speakers contribute authentic material;
4. contributions are verified;
5. underrepresented language material accumulates;
6. the archive becomes more valuable over time.

## Long-term vision

Ogbifọ should evolve from a language experience into a Yorùbá language intelligence layer capable of powering:

- education
- cultural archives
- interpretation
- community knowledge systems
- research
- media
- public services
- conversational interfaces

Direction, not MVP: ease of writing Yorùbá digitally. Tone-marked typing and chat should feel as easy as speaking it. The MVP provides assisted tone-mark entry for contributions (D-015); a full keyboard or input method is a later product.

Any use of Archive material beyond the Ogbifọ experience needs a new product decision and new contributor consent.

> Scope: Ogbifọ is a consumer language and cultural archive/interpreter product, not a data-engine business, and the MVP has no dataset-selling model. See `PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md`. The consumer revenue model is not yet defined.
