# Ogbifọ — Progression System

## Philosophy

Progress should reinforce practice and preservation. It should not gamify cultural identity.

## Rewards

Potential rewards: tokens, rank progression, deck unlocks, archive contributor status, Pioneer status, achievements.

The prototype already associates cards with token values and uses score to increase the user's score balance.

## Rank system

Use titles carefully. The product language uses the idea of "earning the title of Ogbifọ."

Ranks should represent contribution/competence rather than social status.

Hierarchy (`PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md` D-025): Listener, Reader, Interpreter, Keeper, Ogbifọ.

Do not finalize rank thresholds until product analytics are available.

## Pioneer

A Pioneer can be associated with contributing to underrepresented languages or archive gaps. This should reward preservation rather than popularity.

> No leaderboards or competitive ranking in the MVP (`PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md` D-009).
