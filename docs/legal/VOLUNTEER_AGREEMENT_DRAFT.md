## 2. Reviewing Cards

- A Card needs **two different named reviewers** before any learner sees it. I will not review a Card I wrote.
- I will say plainly if a Card is wrong, unclear, rude, or from a dialect I do not speak. I will not approve what I am unsure of.
- My approval is my own judgement as a speaker. It is not a guarantee, and the product never calls a Card "certified" or "authoritative".

## 4. What I contribute

- I keep my copyright in my own work (corrections, notes, annotations). I give the Project a **non-exclusive, worldwide licence**, for as long as the Project runs, to use them in the Ogbifọ Reading Room and Archive. The Project will not license them to anyone else. I can withdraw by writing to [contact]; the Project will stop using them in new releases.
- I will not copy text from books, dictionaries, websites, songs or other sources into Cards unless the Project tells me the source is free to use and I note where it came from.
